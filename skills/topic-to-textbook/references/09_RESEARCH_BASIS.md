# Research Basis for the Pedagogy Layer

This file records the external research basis used to strengthen v2. It is not a claim that every technique should be applied mechanically.

## Retrieval practice

McDermott, K. B. (2021). Practicing Retrieval Facilitates Learning. Annual Review of Psychology, 72, 609-633.
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33006925/

Karpicke, J. D., & Blunt, J. R. (2011). Retrieval practice produces more learning than elaborative studying with concept mapping. Science, 331(6018), 772-775.
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21252317/

Use in Skill: short retrieval opportunities and meaningful recall, not quiz spam.

## Worked examples, cognitive load, and guidance fading

Sweller, J., Ayres, P., & Kalyuga, S. (2011). Cognitive Load Theory. Springer.
https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-1-4419-8126-4

Sweller, J., van Merriënboer, J. J. G., & Paas, F. (2019). Cognitive Architecture and Instructional Design: 20 Years Later. Educational Psychology Review.
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-019-09465-5

Use in Skill: worked examples for novices, then completion problems and independent practice; avoid permanent over-scaffolding.

## Self-explanation

Renkl, A., Stark, R., Gruber, H., & Mandl, H. (1998). Learning from Worked-Out Examples: The Effects of Example Variability and Elicited Self-Explanations.
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9514690/

Use in Skill: selective prompts where causal / procedural reasoning matters, not a mandatory prompt after every section.

## Mastery learning

Persky, A. M., & others. A Practical Review of Mastery Learning. American Journal of Pharmaceutical Education.
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35027359/

Use in Skill: formative assessment, corrective activity and retest when the textbook is used for mastery-oriented learning.

## Multiple representation and accessibility

CAST (2024). Universal Design for Learning Guidelines version 3.0.
https://udlguidelines.cast.org/

Use in Skill: prior-knowledge connection, multiple representation options, transfer/generalization, accessible materials and graduated support.

## Design caution

The research above does not imply universal effect sizes across every topic, age or task. The Skill therefore treats these as design tools selected according to domain, learner level and task, not rigid mandatory widgets.
