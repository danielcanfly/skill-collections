# Independence Boundary

The wrapper must preserve the canonical role boundary rather than flattening all capabilities into one self-approving agent.

## Formal candidate audit

For the strongest independence, use a fresh session/worker with only:

1. the standalone Audit OS v6 package or its preserved extracted tree,
2. the exact candidate artifact,
3. the required admission receipt and external review evidence,
4. any source access explicitly permitted by the audit protocol.

Do not give that audit worker dispatcher or repair tooling unless the canonical standalone-vs-embedded rule says those shared capabilities are genuinely required for the task.

## Prohibited substitutions

- Research Builder self-check ≠ Candidate Auditor evidence.
- A PASS field filled by the builder ≠ external stage proof.
- Same-session role renaming ≠ independence.
- Repairing a candidate during a read-only formal audit ≠ audit.
- Reviewing a different candidate hash ≠ reviewing the submitted candidate.

## Embedded Audit OS

Use the embedded Audit OS when shared schemas, migration rules, repair generation, or global reconciliation are legitimately needed, exactly as the canonical v6 documentation states. Embedded location:

`../engines/UNIVERSAL_RESEARCH_ORCHESTRATION_OS_v10_PROOF_CARRYING_RESEARCH_CONSTRUCTION/modules/audit_os_v6_proof_carrying_construction/`

Even then, preserve reviewer identity/session separation and all exact-SHA/external-review contracts.
