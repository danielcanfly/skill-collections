# Audit Output Contract v4

The audit output must include `AUDIT_SUMMARY.json`, one receipt per registered validator and `FINDINGS_REGISTER.json`.

Required summary fields include exact candidate SHA, Audit OS 6.0.0, orchestration contract v10, admission classification, independent auditor identity, six dimension scores and findings. `MERGE_READY` requires every dimension 100 and zero open P0/P1/P2 findings. Production promotion then verifies exact wrapper construction without weakening the audit result.
