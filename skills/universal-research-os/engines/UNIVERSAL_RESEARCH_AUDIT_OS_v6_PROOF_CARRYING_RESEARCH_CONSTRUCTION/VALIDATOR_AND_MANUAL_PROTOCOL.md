# Validator and Independent Review Protocol

The runner hashes the candidate tree before and after every registered validator. Any mutation is a validator-trust failure.

The external auditor reviews all retained direct-support material clauses, not a sample. Source identities, source snapshots, typed locators, excerpt hashes, domain compatibility, transfer limits, unsupported clauses, and chronology must be independently verified. Candidate-authored receipts are self-check inputs only.
