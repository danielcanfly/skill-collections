# Universal Evidence-backed Case Standard

## Case types
Only:
- evidence-backed
- illustrative

## Evidence-backed requirements
- identifiable context
- source IDs
- decision/problem as it existed at the time
- what was known at the time
- method or framework used
- mechanism
- outcome
- decision-quality interpretation where applicable
- alternative explanation or counterfactual
- transfer limit

## Illustrative scenarios
Useful for teaching and retrieval, but:
- clearly labelled
- do not count toward the case minimum
- cannot support empirical claims

## Framework coverage
Every major framework needs:
- limitation
- misuse/failure mode
- counterexample or boundary case
- When Not to Use
