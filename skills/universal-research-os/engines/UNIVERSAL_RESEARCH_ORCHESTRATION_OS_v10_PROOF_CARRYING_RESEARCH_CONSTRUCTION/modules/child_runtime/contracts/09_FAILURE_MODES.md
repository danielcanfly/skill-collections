# Universal Failure Modes and Recovery

## Cannot read package
Stop. Do not guess the topic or invent candidate topics.

## Topic brief is only a title
The master dispatcher must create a content blueprint before launching the child. A process-only child pack is invalid.

## Single long report returned
Reject. The final package requires research, knowledge, QA and handoff trees.

## Source count reached with duplicates
Reject. Count independent evidence families, not URLs.

## Unverified source supports claim
Remove it from accepted evidence and cascade the change.

## Source only supports one clause
Split/narrow the claim or use partial/contextual support.

## Child creates another child's concept
Record overlap and proposed owner; do not create a duplicate canonical node.

## Validator warning
Repair it or document a justified waiver for independent review. Do not silently ignore it.

## No file-generation ability
Output every required file separately and state that ZIP packaging is unavailable. Do not collapse deliverables into a summary.

## Research is unfinished
Mark gaps and confidence honestly. Do not fabricate completion or inflate evidence.
