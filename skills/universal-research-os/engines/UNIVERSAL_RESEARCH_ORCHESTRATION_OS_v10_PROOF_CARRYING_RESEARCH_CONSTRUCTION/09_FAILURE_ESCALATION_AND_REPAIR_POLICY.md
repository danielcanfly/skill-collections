# Failure Escalation and Repair Policy

- Evidence or source identity failure: repair full affected edge population.
- Compiler proof missing: return to construction stage, not prose patching.
- Runtime contract mismatch: block all active handoffs until canonical runtime rebuild.
- Protected surface drift: reject repair return.
- Global upstream binding failure: do not materialise global surfaces.
- Repeated same-class failure after one repair: escalate to handoff/compiler defect, not another manual patch loop.
